# Satu Ayam yang Sama, v2: narration and shot plan (following the reference's flow)

**Reference flow:** "The Difference Between Rich And Poor" (uQ5XctzP-2M, 73 s).
- We mapped it word by word into 14 beats with their timings (below).
- The narration here is our own Indonesian wording, not a translation. It has the same beats and timing, with our setting: a kampung, a whirlwind, Indonesian classes.
- Video length is about 73 s at 30 fps, roughly 2,190 frames.

**About the joke:** it echoes the 2021 "kampung miliarder" story (Desa Sumurgeneng, Tuban): villagers sold their sawah for a refinery project and bought many new cars. The tone stays friendly, laughing with them. Brand names appear only in the narration; the cars on screen are generic, with no logos.

## Effects used (only where the beat needs them, see effects-toolkit.md)
- **Beat 2:** storm mood, rain, whirlwind.
- **Beats 3 and 5:** night, bonfire, firelight on faces.
- **Beat 4:** dust puff when the chickens land.
- **Beat 14:** zoom out through the clouds to the map.
- **Not used:** cracks, hat pop, window light, pendant lamps. They have no beat that needs them.
